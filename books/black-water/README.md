# Black Water

A noir-tinged crime novel set in Houston, Texas, October 1985, generated by AIRowling.

**[novel.md](./novel.md)** — 29,837 words, 13 chapters.

Detective Don Redman of the HPD Southeast substation pulls the body of a wildcatter named Roy Tolbert from the ship channel on a Tuesday morning with a Rolex Submariner missing from a wrist that still has the tan line. His partner Jimmy Dixon finds a chrome lighter engraved R.T. in a locked tackle box on a subsidiary dredging vessel. Lieutenant Mickey Bordelon signs off on eight years of security permits for the boat that dumped Tolbert into the bayou. Somewhere in a walk-in freezer behind the Cattlemen's steakhouse, a leather ledger holds enough signatures to close the case.

This is the second build of this manuscript (v2). The first shipped with silent defects — a merged character-name (Wanda used for both Jimmy's pregnant wife and the bartender witness), a triple-rendered phone call, and a final chapter that dropped the promised braided arcs. v2 caught each defect either at build time (via the P1–P10 corpus-audit fixes wired into the pipeline) or through a targeted editor pass that used AIRowling's internal `_stub_fill_level2` to regenerate the finale with all seven braided arcs converging.
