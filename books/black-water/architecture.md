## Title
**Black Water**

## Premise
When wildcatter Roy Tolbert is found murdered in Buffalo Bayou, Detective Don Redman uncovers a conspiracy linking the death to a dredging company owned by the father of his daughter’s boyfriend, forcing Don to choose between protecting his family’s fragile peace and exposing a corruption ring protected by his own precinct lieutenant, all while racing to solve the cold case of a missing girl who may have been the original witness.

## Themes
corruption, paternal protection, institutional betrayal

## Voice
close third, gritty and procedural, grounded in sensory detail and dry, understated humor.

## Characters
**Don Redman**: protagonist — wants to solve the murder without endangering his daughter — ex-Rice linebacker, moves with a limp, speaks rarely but listens intensely — eyes: gray — hair: black with gray temples — age: 42
  Active Arc: In Ch07, Don ignores direct orders from Lt. Bordelon to drop the dredging angle, choosing instead to follow a lead to the Ship Channel alone, establishing his defiance of corrupt authority.

**Jimmy Dixon**: partner — wants to keep his new fatherhood secret while maintaining professional loyalty to Don — ex-Marine, thin, deadpan delivery, wears cheap suits — eyes: brown — hair: black — age: 34
  Active Arc: In Ch09, Jimmy intercepts a threat from Wade Hollister’s men aimed at Don, taking a hit to his shoulder to buy Don time to escape, proving his loyalty transcends his personal fears.

**Wanda Freeman**: bartender — wants to survive the cleanup crew by providing information only to those who earn her trust — sharp-eyed, chain-smokes, remembers every face — eyes: green — hair: red — age: 50
  Active Arc: In Ch11, Wanda actively leads Don to the hidden ledger in the Cattlemen’s walk-in freezer, risking her safety to provide the crucial evidence needed to link Hollister to the murder.

**Lt. Mickey Bordelon**: antagonist — wants to bury the dredging scandal to protect his pension and connections — smooth-talking, bureaucratic, uses paperwork as a weapon — eyes: blue — hair: blonde — age: 48
  Active Arc: In Ch05, Bordelon fabricates evidence to frame a local junkie for Tolbert’s murder, attempting to close the case prematurely and eliminate Don as a threat.

**Wade Hollister**: secondary antagonist — wants to maintain his dredging contracts regardless of the bodies left behind — aggressive, loud, drives a white Cadillac — eyes: brown — hair: gray — age: 55
  Active Arc: In Ch12, Wade attempts to bribe Don directly at his home, offering Cassie’s college fund in exchange for silence, raising the stakes from professional to familial.

## Settings
**HPD Southeast Substation**: smells of stale coffee and wet wool; fluorescent lights hum with a dying buzz — the bureaucratic heart of the investigation.
**Cattlemen’s Steakhouse**: sounds of clinking glass and low jazz; the air is thick with cigar smoke and expensive perfume — the place where secrets are traded.
**Buffalo Bayou**: feels oppressive and humid; the water is black and oily, reflecting the city’s neon glow — the site of the discovery and the final confrontation.
**Ship Channel**: smells of rust and diesel fuel; the cranes loom like skeletal giants against the twilight sky — the industrial artery of Houston’s corruption.
**Don’s Apartment**: feels cramped and lonely; the radiator clanks loudly in the night, echoing the silence of his empty bed — his sanctuary and his prison.

## Plot Points
1. (setup, ch01) Don identifies Tolbert’s body and notices the missing Rolex and tan line — establishes the murder’s precision.
2. (setup, ch02) Don visits the Cattlemen’s booth; Wanda refuses to name the patron, citing fear — introduces the witness and the intimidation.
3. (ticking clock, ch03) Bordelon assigns Don to mundane traffic duty, effectively sidelining him for 48 hours — the deadline begins.
4. (foreshadow, ch04) Don visits his daughter Cassie; Trent Hollister is there, and Don feels a visceral distrust of the Hollister family — links the personal arc.
5. (midpoint, ch05) Jimmy finds the engraved lighter ‘R.T.’ on a dredging boat during a routine check; Don realizes the victim and the dredger are connected — the twist.
6. (reversal, ch06) Don confronts Bordelon about the lighter; Bordelon threatens Don’s pension and hints at Cassie’s safety — the stakes spike.
7. (escalation, ch07) Don goes rogue, visiting the Ship Channel alone; he is ambushed by Hollister’s men — the physical danger begins.
8. (deepening, ch08) Jimmy reveals his pregnancy news to Don, seeking emotional support; Don realizes Jimmy is his only true ally — strengthens the bond.
9. (crisis, ch09) Jimmy takes a bullet meant for Don during the ambush; Don escapes but Jimmy is hospitalized — the cost of defiance.
10. (investigation, ch10) Don returns to the Cattlemen’s, threatening Wanda to speak; she reveals Amber Salazar was also on the boat — connects the cold case.
11. (breakthrough, ch11) Wanda gives Don the location of the ledger in the freezer; Don retrieves it, confirming Hollister’s guilt — the evidence is secured.
12. (confrontation, ch12) Wade Hollister tries to bribe Don at his home; Don refuses and arrests Wade on the spot — the climax of the personal arc.
13. (resolution, ch13) Bordelon is arrested based on the ledger’s financial trails; Don visits Jimmy in the hospital — the institutional defeat.
14. (aftermath, ch14) Don tells Cassie about Trent’s father’s arrest; Cassie leaves, heartbroken but understanding — the irrevocable loss.
15. (ending, ch15) Rita gives birth; Don watches the sunrise over the channel, the case closed but the city unchanged — the thematic conclusion.

## Chapters
1. Don identifies Tolbert’s body in the morgue, noting the precise nature of the kill and the missing watch, setting the tone of a professional crime.
2. Don questions Wanda at Cattlemen’s; she shuts him down, revealing the city’s code of silence and her own fear.
3. Bordelon reassigns Don to paperwork, creating a bureaucratic obstacle course that Don must navigate to keep the case alive.
4. Don visits Cassie; the presence of Trent Hollister triggers Don’s protective instincts and suspicion of the Hollister name.
5. Jimmy discovers the lighter on the dredging boat, realizing the connection between the victim and the dredging industry.
6. Don confronts Bordelon with the lighter; the lieutenant’s reaction confirms his involvement in the cover-up.
7. Don investigates the Ship Channel alone, encountering violence from Hollister’s enforcers, showing his determination despite danger.
8. Jimmy shares the pregnancy news with Don, deepening their brotherhood and highlighting the contrast between their personal lives.
9. Jimmy is shot while protecting Don; the incident raises the stakes and forces Don to act faster and harder.
10. Don pressures Wanda again; she reveals Amber Salazar’s connection to the boat, merging the two cases.
11. Wanda helps Don retrieve the ledger from the restaurant’s freezer, providing the tangible proof needed for an arrest.
12. Wade Hollister attempts to bribe Don at his home; Don rejects the offer and initiates the arrest, resolving the immediate conflict.
13. Bordelon is arrested as the financial evidence implicates him; Don reflects on the cost of justice within a corrupt system.
14. Don informs Cassie of the arrest; the conversation marks the end of their childhood innocence and the beginning of adult complexity.
15. The story ends with Rita’s labor and Don’s quiet observation of the city, balancing personal joy with professional exhaustion.

## Pacing
Tension builds steadily through bureaucratic obstruction and personal threats. The midpoint (Ch05) shifts the genre from procedural to thriller with the discovery of the lighter. The climax occurs in Ch12 with the confrontation at Don’s home. The resolution in Ch13-15 provides emotional closure without erasing the systemic issues.

## Irrevocable Loss
Don loses the innocence of his relationship with his daughter, Cassie. By arresting Wade Hollister, he destroys her romantic relationship and forces her to see the ugly reality of the world he protects. She will not forget this betrayal, and the trust between them is fractured forever, even if they eventually heal. Additionally, Don loses his partner Jimmy’s health temporarily, and Jimmy’s secret joy is tainted by the violence of their work.
